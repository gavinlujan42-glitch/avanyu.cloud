One project, four power ledgers.

Published September 14, 2026 · 8:39 AM MDT

ORANGE · GRID-BOUNDARY WATCH

A timestamped civic review of the September 8 county tape records Oracle describing current El Paso Electric service for construction and house/backup power, plus an EPE easement. Full operating grid power remains only a contingency if the Bloom air permit fails.

Attributable public record

The county-tape review records Oracle saying it buys EPE electricity for construction and house/backup loads. It also records discussion of an EPE easement. The underlying service and easement instruments were not located publicly, so their capacity, route, term and cost allocation remain unverified.

Oracle’s primary design remains up to 2.45 GW of natural-gas-fed Bloom fuel cells. Its separate statement that a grid provider may be needed if the air permit fails describes a future branch, not the present construction arrangement.

Limits and disputed framing

Critics characterize any EPE service as inconsistent with “independent power.” The project’s public materials speak mainly to the completed campus and customer-rate effects. Construction power, backup power, full operating load and renewable matching are different scopes. No public document reviewed establishes full-load grid service, cost shifting, misconduct or corruption.

Infrastructure judgment

Governance should maintain four auditable ledgers: construction service; house/backup service; operating load; renewable-energy matching. The EPE easement is now the priority record because its permitted facilities and assignment rights could distinguish routine temporary service from durable optionality.

Control requests

  1. Publish the EPE easement, legal description and route map.
  2. Publish utility-service agreements, meters and demand limits.
  3. Separate construction, backup and operating-load forecasts.
  4. Disclose any interconnection study and who pays for required upgrades.
  5. Preserve separate water ledgers for construction, initial fills and operations.
