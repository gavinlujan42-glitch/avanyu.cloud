Water, infrastructure and governance edition

Renewable matching enters the critical path.

Published September 10, 2026 · 8:49 AM MDT

ORANGE · UNCHANGED

Oracle has announced an RFP for 2 GW of new renewable generation in New Mexico, while county appointments to the Project Jupiter monitoring committee moved to September 22. The RFP does not remove Jupiter’s near-term gas, pipeline, air-permit or water-accounting dependencies.

Verified developments

2-GW renewable solicitation

Oracle says the September 8 RFP seeks New Mexico solar, wind, geothermal and other renewable projects deliverable between 2027 and 2031. Selection factors include timing, project maturity, technical viability, capacity and growth potential.

The announcement establishes procurement intent, not delivered electricity. No public RFP package located by publication time identifies bid deadlines, interconnection nodes, storage requirements, hourly-matching rules, transmission arrangements or executed PPAs.

County oversight delayed

The planned September 8 appointment of the 11-member Community Monitoring Committee was moved to September 22. A final roster and affiliation disclosures therefore remain unverified.

Infrastructure remains conditional

Oracle’s own release conditions projected jobs, taxes, investment and economic impact on the air permit and pipeline being approved as originally planned. The onsite Bloom fuel-cell design still requires natural gas; renewable-energy matching is a separate layer unless future contracts show direct, time-matched delivery.

Water and governance assessment

No new public meter ledger or copy of Oracle’s August 31 acre-feet report surfaced. Construction withdrawals, initial system fills, operating cooling/power water and potable workforce use must remain separate accounting categories.

The two-week committee delay is a governance gap, not proof the oversight mechanism will fail. The key tests are membership independence, technical expertise, disclosure/recusal rules, record access, meeting transparency and the authority to validate environmental claims.

No evidence reviewed today establishes corruption. NMDOJ’s water and air arguments remain litigated positions, not court findings.

Priority questions

  • Will the renewable portfolio provide hourly physical supply, annual matching or certificate-based accounting?
  • Where are the RFP instructions, deadlines, interconnection requirements and evaluation weights?
  • What pipeline route and land approvals will support the gas-fed Bloom system?
  • When will the August 31 water-use report and underlying OSE meter ledger be public?
  • Who is appointed September 22, and what information can the committee compel or independently validate?
