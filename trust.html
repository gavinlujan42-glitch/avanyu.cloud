Platform rule

Identity, policy enforcement, least privilege, provenance, client isolation, audit evidence, observability and rollback are architectural requirements. RAG context is not authority.

Consequential systems

For water, government and critical-infrastructure workflows, consequential judgments remain with authorized people unless a narrow technical action has been explicitly authorized under the tier model.
