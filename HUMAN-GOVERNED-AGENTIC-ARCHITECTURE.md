# Avanyu Cloud Human-Governed Agentic Architecture

**Doctrine version: 2026-09-12**

Avanyu Cloud is the secure execution and evidence plane for human-governed agentic services.

**Client → Contract → Scope → Policy → Identity → Agent → Evidence → Human Authority → Client**

## Governing rule
**AI supplies velocity. People retain authority.** AI assistants and agents may research, analyze, generate and, when explicitly authorized, execute bounded actions. RAG context is evidence-bearing context, never independent authority.

## Authorization tiers
- Tier 0: Observe.
- Tier 1: Recommend.
- Tier 2: Generate change artifacts without execution.
- Tier 3: Human-approved execution.
- Tier 4: Pre-authorized autonomous remediation limited to reversible, low-blast-radius actions with rollback and complete logging.

## Platform controls
Identity-aware access, policy enforcement, model/input safeguards, least privilege, client isolation, provenance-aware retrieval, tamper-resistant audit evidence, observability and rollback are architectural requirements.

## Commercial separation
Professional services are packaged as advisory hours, architecture sprints, assessments, managed services or fixed deliverables. Cloud/model consumption is metered separately or transparently included. Machine processing time is not billed as if it were human professional-service labor.

## Water and critical-infrastructure use
For consequential water, government and critical-infrastructure workflows, AI remains assistive unless an action has been explicitly authorized under the tier model. Regulatory determinations and other consequential judgments remain with authorized people.
